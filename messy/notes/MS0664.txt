PLAN:
1. Order bilateral lower extremity arterial duplex with ankle-brachial indices to further characterize disease severity and localization.
2. Continue atorvastatin for lipid management and plaque stabilization; will coordinate with PCP regarding optimization.
3. Recommend structured walking/exercise program to improve collateral circulation and claudication distance.
4. Discussed importance of blood pressure control and glycemic monitoring given vascular risk profile; recommend PCP follow-up for these.
5. Continue CPAP for OSA, continue levothyroxine and omeprazole as prescribed.
6. Follow-up in clinic after duplex imaging to discuss results and further management, which may include consideration of medical therapy versus intervention depending on findings.
