ASSESSMENT AND PLAN: Mr. Alexander is a 72-year-old male with an incidentally discovered pulmonary nodule. Given his overall low-risk smoking history, as detailed above, and imaging characteristics, we will proceed with interval surveillance imaging per Fleischner Society guidelines. We discussed the general concept of lung cancer screening programs and their role in patients with significant tobacco exposure history, and explained why his case differs. He was counseled on symptoms that should prompt earlier follow-up including hemoptysis, unexplained weight loss, or worsening dyspnea. Continue current asthma regimen. Continue GERD management with PPI, reinforce lifestyle measures. Continue CPAP adherence for OSA. CKD stage 3 to remain under nephrology follow-up. Will obtain follow-up chest imaging in approximately 6-12 months and correlate with nephrology-recommended contrast precautions given his renal function. Return to clinic sooner if new symptoms arise.
